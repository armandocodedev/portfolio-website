MVP #1 - Reserva

Building operations without the WhatsApp chaos.

Reserva is an early MVP for buildings that need a cleaner way to manage shared amenity reservations, maintenance reports, quotes, approvals, and repair history.

Request pilot Back to Lab

What It Tests

Amenity Reservations

Residents need simple rules and visible availability for shared spaces and resources.

  • Barbecue / parrillero
  • Laundry room
  • Gym or fitness room
  • Event room, terrace, pool, or sports court
  • Guest parking and moving elevator slots

Maintenance Tracking

Administrators and committees need a clear trail from problem to closure.

  1. Report with photo and description
  2. Validate the issue
  3. Compare provider quotes
  4. Approve with history
  5. Coordinate and close with evidence

Validation Questions

The goal is not to pretend this is already proven. The goal is to find out which pain is strongest: amenity reservations, maintenance traceability, or both together.

Who needs it? Residents, committees, building administrators, or property managers.
What hurts most? Shared space booking, repair tracking, approvals, provider quotes, or building rules.
Would they pay? Testing a pilot range of USD 150-300 per building/month plus setup.

Follow The Build

I am launching Reserva through Armandocode first. If the MVP earns real traction, it can later become its own product brand. Until then, the test stays lightweight: build, post, collect feedback, improve, and decide.

Privacy & Cookies

How this portfolio uses analytics and stores consent choices.

Analytics

This site uses PostHog only after you accept analytics tracking. It measures page visits, campaign sources, project selections, and contact clicks. An anonymous browser identifier is stored in local storage to recognize returning visits.

Website analytics does not capture form contents or record sessions. Survey answers and any email address you choose to provide are handled separately as described below. I do not sell your data.

Survey & Email Updates

The audience survey is hosted by PostHog in its US region and loads when you choose to take it. You can vote without accepting website analytics or providing an email address.

PostHog stores the answers you submit. If you explicitly choose email updates, it also stores the email address you provide with that response. This permission is separate from analytics consent.

Your email will be used for occasional Armando Code releases and content updates. To withdraw permission or request deletion of your survey response, email armandocodedev@gmail.com. Withdrawing email permission does not require changing your analytics choice.

Your Choice

You can reject analytics when the cookie banner appears. If you accepted before and want to choose again, use the button below.
